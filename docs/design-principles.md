# Design Principles

1. **Human-readable first.** Commands should be easy to type and explain.
2. **Rules over repeated prompts.** Store durable knowledge in the specification.
3. **Portable by design.** Avoid coupling the language to one AI provider.
4. **State is explicit.** Persist important continuity outside model memory.
5. **Human judgment stays in the loop.** Commands accelerate creation; they do not replace review.
6. **Start small.** Add abstractions only after a workflow proves repeatable.
7. **Outputs can multiply.** One approved master asset may branch into captions, motion, carousels, interactive experiences and products.
